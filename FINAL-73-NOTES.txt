FINAL-73 — WEEKLY 50-SLOT QUEUES
- 7 weekday queues, maximum 50 lots per day.
- New lot receives next free position automatically (1/50 ... 50/50).
- Global LOT # remains unique and is assigned automatically by server.
- Delete/archive closes the gap and reindexes later positions automatically.
- Moving a lot to another weekday reindexes both old and new weekday queues.
- Admin weekday filter shows occupied count and next free position.
- Live Auction uses only today's Dubai weekday queue and advances automatically.
- Lots from other weekdays cannot hijack today's Live screen.
